Tested on a Sony A80J: with a unique certificate subject per client, pairing client B left client A connected. Shipped builds with unique client names but a shared CN ('FreeRemote') still lost pairings, so the TV tells clients apart by certificate subject. Cancelling a pairing from our side leaves the code dialog up on the TV until Back is pressed.
