# Open Remote

A free, open source TV remote for iPhone, iPad and Mac. No subscriptions, no ads, no tracking, no account.

Works with TVs on your Wi-Fi network:

- **Google TV and Android TV**: Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Sharp, Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia Shield
- **Roku**: Roku TVs, sticks and boxes
- **Samsung**: Tizen smart TVs (2016 and newer)
- **LG**: webOS smart TVs

## Support

**My TV isn't found.** Make sure your phone or Mac is on the same Wi-Fi network as the TV and the TV is on. On iPhone, check that Local Network access is allowed for Open Remote (Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network). You can also add a TV by its IP address, which is usually shown in the TV's network settings.

**Google TV / Android TV asks for a code.** Tap Pair in the app and type the 6-character code shown on your TV. Codes use the numbers 0–9 and the letters A–F.

**Roku doesn't respond to buttons.** On the Roku, go to Settings → System → Advanced system settings → Control by mobile apps and set Network access to Default or Permissive.

**Samsung or LG.** The first time you pair, the TV asks whether to allow the connection. Choose Allow (or Accept) with your regular remote.

**Volume buttons do nothing.** If your TV's sound goes through a soundbar or receiver, volume is controlled by that device. Many soundbars still follow the TV's volume commands over HDMI.

Still stuck? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/iamnbutler/open-tv-remote/issues) and describe your TV model.
