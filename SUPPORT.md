# Getting help

1. Check the [troubleshooting guide](https://github.com/iamnbutler/open-tv-remote/wiki/Troubleshooting) in the wiki. Each TV brand also has its own setup page.
2. Search [existing issues](https://github.com/iamnbutler/open-tv-remote/issues?q=is%3Aissue) in case someone already hit the same problem.
3. If you're still stuck, [open an issue](https://github.com/iamnbutler/open-tv-remote/issues/new/choose). Include your TV's brand and model, since most problems are specific to one kind of TV.

This is a free side project with no paid support, so answers may take a while.
